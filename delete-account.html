Delete your Ibadah Tracker account

This permanently deletes:

  • your sign-in (email and password), and
  • all your tracker data in the cloud: every day you logged, Quran & Nafal, and your monthly percentages.

It cannot be undone. If you may want your data later, sign in to the tracker and use Download Backup File first.

Data in local mode (not signed in) is only on your device and is not affected; remove it with Wipe Data or by uninstalling the app. See the privacy page.

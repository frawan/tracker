Privacy

Ibadah Tracker (website and Android app) · Last updated 20 October 2026

Ibadah Tracker is a personal tool for logging your prayers, Quran and Nafal. It keeps as little about you as it can.

Two ways to use it

Local mode (not signed in): everything stays on your device, in the browser or the app. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else.

Cloud sync (signed in): your data is stored in Google Firebase so it can sync between your phone and laptop.

What is stored when you sign in

  • Your email address, and your password handled by Firebase Authentication. The app never sees or stores your password itself.
  • Your tracker entries: for each day you log, the five prayers (alone / Jama'at), and whether you did Quran and Nafal.
  • The monthly percentages you enter under "Update Previous Months Data".

What is not collected

No analytics, no advertising, no location, no contacts, no tracking across apps or websites. Your data is not sold or shared.

Who can see your data

Only you, when signed in. The database rules only allow each account to read and write its own record. The data is held by Google Firebase (Google Cloud) as the service provider; see Firebase privacy and security.

Deleting your data

  • Account and all cloud data: sign in and press Delete my account, or use the Delete account page, which works without the app. Deletion is immediate and permanent.
  • Local-mode data: use Wipe Data & Reset Timeline, clear the site data in your browser, or uninstall the app.
  • Backup files you download are yours; delete them like any other file.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/frawan/tracker/issues. Please don't post your password or other private details there.

Ibadah Tracker for Android

Track your five prayers (alone / Jama'at), Quran and Nafal. Works offline; sign in to sync with your laptop. Free, no ads.

Android 7.0 or newer · phones and tablets

How to install

  1. 1Tap Download the app on your phone. If Chrome warns about the file, choose Download anyway.
  2. 2Open the downloaded file. The first time, Android asks to allow installing unknown apps from Chrome: tap Settings, switch it on, then go back.
  3. 3Tap Install, then Open. Sign in with your tracker email to bring your data across.

Updating: download again from this page and install. The new version replaces the old one and your data stays.
